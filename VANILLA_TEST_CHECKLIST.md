# Vanilla set: headset test checklist

**If something's wrong, tell the build lane which gun and which step.**

This covers what's installed today, 2026-09-14. Nothing parked is here (the free full load on a picked-up gun, and
the Machine Gun's grenade launcher being moved to Vanilla+), and nothing still unbuilt (bloom, smoke volumes,
chunks).

---

## Before you start

- **Getting the guns.** A new game starts like Doom: fists, the **M4A3** and the **9mm Handgun**, each with a full
  magazine, and 50 bullets.
  - **Everything at once:** turn on **"Start with the whole test arsenal instead"** in the weapon set menu, then start
    a new game. It gives all 16 guns, the Double Barrel included.
  - **Pickups in a map come as pairs.** The first time you walk over one you get its main-hand gun, the second time
    its off-hand gun:
    - Pistol: M4A3, then 9mm Handgun
    - Shotgun: M37A2, then Doom pump
    - Super Shotgun: Super Shotgun, then Bullpup Pump
    - Chaingun: Chaingun, then Machine Gun
    - Rocket Launcher: Rocket Launcher, then RPG
    - Plasma Rifle: Plasma Rifle, then Plasma Carbine
    - BFG: BFG, then Heavy BFG
    - Chainsaw: Chainsaw, then Heavy Chainsaw
  - The **Double Barrel** isn't on any pickup now. Get it from the test arsenal.
- **The moves every gun uses:**
  - **Drop the magazine:** press the drop-mag button on the hand holding the gun, or take hold of the magazine and pull
    it out.
  - **Draw a fresh one:** squeeze inside the pouch.
  - **Put one back:** open your hand inside the pouch.
  - **Seat it:** bring it to the mouth of the well and push until it catches.
  - **Rack:** take the slide, pull it back, let go.
- **Two-handed guns** (Chaingun, Machine Gun, Rocket Launcher, RPG, BFG, Heavy BFG) fire only while your other hand
  holds the support grip.
- **A hand busy working the other gun puts its own gun away.** It comes back the moment that hand is free.

## The 16 guns

### M4A3 (main hand)
- **Get it:** you start with it.
- **Reload:**
  1. Fire it dry. The slide locks back.
  2. Drop the magazine.
  3. Squeeze in the pouch for a fresh one and seat it.
  4. Rack the slide.
- **Right:**
  - 15 shots from a dry reload.
  - A reload before it runs dry keeps the chambered round, so 16 with no rack.
  - The .45's rounder, orange flash.
  - Brass flies out to the right on every shot.
  - After about **3 shots as fast as it fires**, a thin smoke ribbon curls off the muzzle (see "Smoking pistol barrel").
- **A bug:** a 16th shot from a dry reload, or the slide not locking back when it runs empty.

### 9mm Handgun (off hand)
- **Get it:** you start with it.
- **Reload:** as the M4A3, with the other hands.
- **Right:**
  - 15 from dry, 16 with one kept in the chamber.
  - A sharp white flash with a star that turns every shot.
  - Brass flies out on every shot.
  - It smokes after about **8 shots as fast as it fires**.
- **A bug:** its own magazine refuses to seat, or the count is off.

### M37A2 pump (main hand)
- **Get it:** the Shotgun pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:**
  1. Squeeze in the pouch for a shell.
  2. Bring it to the loading port under the gun and open your hand. Repeat, up to 8.
  3. Rack the pump: pull the forend back, then push it home.
- **Right:**
  - It holds 8 in the tube, plus 1 in the chamber if you load after racking.
  - Pulling the pump back throws the hull out; pushing it home feeds the next shell.
  - It doesn't cycle by itself after a shot.
  - A long, narrow gout of fire with a hard white strobe.
  - After about **4 quick shots** it can smoke too.
- **A bug:** it fires twice without a rack, or a rack loses two shells.

### Doom pump (off hand)
- **Get it:** the Shotgun pickup (second), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:** as the M37A2, with the other hands.
- **Right:**
  - 8 + 1.
  - A fat orange fireball with a warm, lingering strobe.
  - The hull flies out when you pull the pump back.
- **A bug:** as the M37A2.

### Super Shotgun (main hand)
- **Get it:** the Super Shotgun pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:**
  1. Break it open: tip the barrels down, or press the drop-mag button.
  2. Turn the open gun over. The hulls fall out (or press drop-mag again).
  3. Squeeze in the pouch for a shell and let it go at the breech. Do that twice.
  4. Close it: push the barrels up, or snap the gun upward.
- **Right:**
  - One pull fires both barrels. With only one shell loaded, one pull fires that one.
  - Doom's own Super Shotgun sounds for fire, open, load and close.
- **A bug:** it fires while open or half-shut, or opening throws the hulls without being turned over.

### Double Barrel (off hand)
- **Get it:** the test arsenal only.
- **Reload:** as the Super Shotgun, with the other hands.
- **Right:**
  - **The new size:** it's smaller now, and its shells are the same size as every other shotgun's.
  - The shell in your hand matches the shells you see in the chambers.
  - The spot where shells go in sits right at the breech.
  - Its own sounds.
- **A bug:** the shell in your hand looks bigger or smaller than the ones in the chambers, or shells won't go in at
  the breech.

### Chaingun (main hand)
- **Get it:** the Chaingun pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:**
  1. Drop the magazine box.
  2. Squeeze in the pouch for a fresh one and seat it.
  3. No rack.
- **Right:**
  - 100 shots from a box.
  - Hold the trigger: about 9 shots a second.
  - **The first two shots of each hold go dead on, then it scatters.**
  - The barrels spin up and down.
  - Brass and belt links fly out.
  - It fires only with your other hand on the support grip.
- **A bug:** it fires one-handed, or the first two shots scatter.

### Machine Gun (off hand)
- **Get it:** the Chaingun pickup (second), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:** none for bullets. It feeds straight from your bullet reserve until that runs out.
- **Right:**
  - The same cadence as the Chaingun, first two shots dead on.
  - Two hands.
  - Brass flies out.
- **A bug:** it stops firing while you still have bullets in reserve.

### Rocket Launcher (main hand)
- **Get it:** the Rocket Launcher pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:**
  1. Pull the rocket rack out, or press drop-mag.
  2. Squeeze in the pouch for a fresh rack and seat it.
- **Right:**
  - 6 rockets.
  - **Holding the trigger fires again about every half second.**
  - **One** fire sound per rocket.
  - Two hands.
  - Vanilla's rocket: same speed and blast.
- **A bug:** a doubled fire sound, one rocket per pull while you hold the trigger, or it firing one-handed.

### RPG (off hand)
- **Get it:** the Rocket Launcher pickup (second), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:** pull the drum out (or press drop-mag), then draw and seat a fresh drum.
- **Right:**
  - 6 rockets.
  - The drum turns a step every shot.
  - It refires while held, with one fire sound, like the Rocket Launcher.
- **A bug:** as the Rocket Launcher, or the drum not turning.

### Plasma Rifle (main hand)
- **Get it:** the Plasma Rifle pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:** pull the cell out (or press drop-mag), then draw and seat a fresh cell.
- **Right:**
  - 50 shots.
  - Hold the trigger and it streams.
  - Let go and there's a short cool-down, about half a second, before it fires again.
  - Vanilla's plasma sound, **once** per shot.
  - No casings.
- **A bug:** a doubled sound, or firing past 50.

### Plasma Carbine (off hand)
- **Get it:** the Plasma Rifle pickup (second), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:** as the Plasma Rifle, with the other hands.
- **Right:** as the Plasma Rifle.
- **A bug:** as the Plasma Rifle.

### BFG (main hand)
- **Get it:** the BFG pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:**
  1. Swing the cover open.
  2. Pull the cell out, or press drop-mag. That only works with the cover open.
  3. Draw and seat a fresh cell.
  4. Swing the cover shut.
- **Right:**
  - 4 shots from a cell.
  - Vanilla's charge sound, then the ball almost a second later.
  - **Holding the trigger charges and fires again**, a shot about every 1.1 seconds.
  - After you let go, it's ready again about 1.7 seconds after the last shot started.
  - It won't fire with the cover open.
  - Two hands.
- **A bug:** it fires with the cover open, fires with no charge wait, or doesn't refire while held.

### Heavy BFG (off hand)
- **Get it:** the BFG pickup (second), or the arsenal.
- **Reload:** there's no cover. Pull the cell out (or press drop-mag), then draw and seat a fresh one.
- **Right:** as the BFG: 4 shots, the charge, refires while held, two hands.
- **A bug:** as the BFG.

### Chainsaw (main hand)
- **Get it:** the Chainsaw pickup (first), or the arsenal.
- **Start it:** pull the ripcord out with your other hand. The engine catches with a start sound, then idles.
- **Right:**
  - It won't cut until started.
  - Trigger down: it cuts, and the idle gives way to the cutting sound.
  - Trigger up: it idles again.
  - Put it away and the engine stops; it needs the cord again next time.
  - It never turns you or drags you toward what it cuts.
- **A bug:** it cuts before the cord is pulled, or keeps idling after it's put away.

### Heavy Chainsaw (off hand)
- **Get it:** the Chainsaw pickup (second), or the arsenal.
- **No ripcord.**
- **Right:**
  - **A steady idle hum while it's in your hand** and the trigger is up.
  - It cuts on the trigger.
  - The hum stops while cutting, when it's put away, or when you die.
- **A bug:** the hum stutters or restarts over and over (the old fault), or it's silent in your hand.

## Today's new things

### Catch-to-equip
- **Pull:** point at a weapon pickup across the room, lock onto it and flick it toward you, as you pull any object.
  In the air it looks like the gun the pulling hand would get.
- **Catch with the main hand:** you get the pair's main-hand gun, put straight into that hand, with Doom's ammo.
- **Catch with the off hand:** the pair's off-hand gun, into the off hand.
- **Already carrying that gun:** the catching hand switches to it and you get the ammo.
- **Miss:** it lands as that weapon's ammo, as Doom's pickup would give it. The chainsaws land as the gun pickup again.
- **Walking over a pickup** still gives the next gun of the pair, as before.
- **Right:** the squeeze that catches doesn't also grab a part of your other gun or draw from the pouch.
- **A bug:** a catch that also starts a pouch draw or a part grab, or the wrong gun for the hand that caught.

### Smoking pistol barrel
- **Try:**
  - Fire the **M4A3** about 3 times as fast as it allows, or the **9mm Handgun** about 8 times.
  - Then wave the gun around.
  - Put it away and draw it straight back.
- **Right:**
  - A thin, lit smoke ribbon curls up off the muzzle.
  - It follows the gun and thins as the barrel cools, with the air shimmering over it.
  - A gun drawn straight back is still warm and still smokes a little.
  - While that hand is busy with the other gun (gun put away) it doesn't smoke.
- **A bug:** smoke hanging where the gun used to be, or smoke after a single shot.

### Refire while held: launchers and BFG
- **Rocket Launcher and RPG:** hold the trigger, and a rocket goes about every half second.
- **BFG and Heavy BFG:** hold the trigger, and it charges and fires about every 1.1 seconds.
- **A bug:** one shot per pull while you hold.

### Pistol magazines: either one in either gun
- **Try:** drop the M4A3's magazine, carry it to the 9mm Handgun, and seat it. Then the other way round.
- **Right:**
  - It seats and fires.
  - Both guns hold 15.
  - A magazine in the hand working a gun takes on that gun's magazine look.
  - Each gun keeps its own magazine look.
- **A bug:** a magazine refusing to seat in the other pistol, or a wrong count after swapping.

### Double Barrel's new size
- **Right:** it's smaller than before, and its shells, loose and in the chambers, match every other shotgun's.
- **A bug:** a size jump when a shell goes in.

### Super Shotgun pickup pair
- **Right:** the first Super Shotgun pickup gives the **Super Shotgun** and the second gives the **Bullpup Pump**. The
  Double Barrel isn't on it.
- **A bug:** the Double Barrel coming from the pickup.
