# The WW2 set, scanned

Brutal Wolfenstein's models, read with our own md3 tool. The body of each model is
the surface that MOVES LEAST across the animation; every other surface is measured
against it. Travel is a centroid's own displacement between frames, so a hinging
part reports its real movement rather than a rotation artifact.
